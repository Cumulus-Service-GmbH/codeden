section: security
---

- **Factory lessons need a trusted source.** On a public repository anyone can comment on a trusted issue or on the factory's pull request, and those comments reach the builder and the reviewer. A reviewer's lessons are now kept only when nobody outside the project's trusted set commented on the work: write access, the account CodeDen uses and the trusted authors are trusted. If GitHub can't be read to check, nothing is kept. A dropped lesson leaves a note on the item. Builder and reviewer briefs also name the people who commented without trust as untrusted input.
- **New lessons are visible before they steer anyone.** Each new lesson is listed once in the daily digest ("New lesson: …, from #12"), and goes into builders' and reviewers' briefs only a day after it was learned. The Lessons panel shows when a waiting lesson starts, so you can forget it first.
