- **The Antigravity CLI as a provider.** CodeDen drives Google's Antigravity CLI (`agy` 1.5.12 or newer) through its ACP server, with your own login from `agy`. It finds `agy` on your PATH or in `~/.local/bin`, checks it on the setup screen, and offers its models with their thinking levels as reasoning effort, plan mode, resume, images and CodeDen's tools. Run `agy` once to sign in before your first session.
