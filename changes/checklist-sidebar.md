- **Project checklists in a pinnable sidebar.** A collapsible right-hand sidebar shows one foldable list per project, each with its progress as done/total (%) and a progress bar. Every item shows its state ([x] done, [~] in progress, [ ] not started), a one-line status, an ETA in your local time and the sessions working on it. Items waiting on you are marked "Needs you" with a link to the session to act in, and a "Changed since last update" section lists what was ticked, added or newly blocked, with times. The sidebar updates live, remembers whether it is pinned or collapsed, and falls back to a slim rail or an overlay when the window is too narrow.
- **The orchestrator keeps the checklist, and you approve it.** The orchestrator creates and updates checklist items with its MCP tools; PMs and agent sessions can only change the status, note, ETA and "needs you" marker of their items. A checklist starts as a draft and is locked once you approve it from its card in the inbox or the sidebar. After that, adding, removing or rewording items needs your approval of a card that shows the exact change, and items added that way are marked as scope additions. Sessions started for an item move it to in progress, done or blocked as they work, wait or finish.
- **Optional plan enforcement.** With "Enforce plan" switched on for an approved checklist (off by default), a PM's request to start work that isn't on the approved list raises an expansion card for you instead of starting.
- **Reviewed plans can be pinned.** Approve the reviewed plan in the sidebar or inbox to pin its SHA and make its tasks the checklist. PM starts outside the pinned scope are refused. The first reviewed plan shows changes to any existing checklist before you approve it. Revisions show the changes for approval while the previous plan stays in force. Rewording a task preserves its progress, linked sessions, and other live details.
