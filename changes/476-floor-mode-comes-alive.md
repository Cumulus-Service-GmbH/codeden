- **The Factory view's Floor mode comes alive.** Each crew figure shows its status on itself (the light on its tip, its mouth and its pose for working, thinking, waiting for approval, blocked, failed, done and idle), breathes, blinks and types in its own rhythm, and wears a paper name tag with its issue number and step. Thought bubbles over the figures say what each live agent is doing, with the provider's mark. Hover or focus a figure for its card; click it to select it and fly the camera there, and to open its item as its roster row does.
- **Walks and moments on the Floor.** Builders walk in from the pallet with their ticket, carry their pull request to the reviewer and walk back, reviewers stamp the crate on its way to the merge queue, merged crates ride the conveyor out of the dock door, and failed work trudges to the repair bay, whose crates offer Retry and Open PR. Opening the view never replays the day. A stopped factory dims the hall and stands everyone still; a draining one hangs a CLOSED sign on intake's door.
- **The Lookout storey in a dark factory.** The scout works at a map table above the Workshop, with eight labelled pods for its area reviewers that light up while each area works, and a glass chute that drops capsules to intake while the scout files. Tabs for Workshop, Lookout and Both count each storey's agents and flag anyone waiting on you.
- **A status board over the dock** shows today's merges, agents working and items waiting on you. Floor now works on phones too, one storey at a time with the crew as a bottom sheet.
