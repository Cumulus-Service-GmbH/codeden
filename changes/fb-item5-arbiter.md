- The Team editor now includes an Arbiter role.
- Factory reviews track their reviewed commits and defer findings according to the project's LOW policy. Disputed findings that reach the review limit go to the Arbiter for a ruling and then return to the reviewer.
- Disputed Arbiter confirmations now open an owner decision. Approved changes can be re-checked after the review limit, and findings in newly changed files stay in scope.
- Sessions without checkpoints can be reviewed again. Documentation changes, exact reverts, and changes without acceptance criteria can supply verification evidence from the current commit.
